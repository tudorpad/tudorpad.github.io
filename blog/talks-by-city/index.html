Here is a list of all the places where I have given a talk as of 12 September 2026. It counts both seminar and conference talks. It felt a little too informal to include in my CV, so I decided to put it here instead.

A few notes on the count. When I gave a series of talks, I counted the whole series as one. Some talks were on Zoom, including two jointly hosted talks (by Bonn and the Perimeter Institute, and by UBC and ETH), and I counted them for both institutions. I group Université Paris Cité, Sorbonne/Jussieu and the Institut Henri Poincaré under Paris, and UC Berkeley and SLMath under Berkeley. Job talks are not included.

The talk in Gdańsk was contributed. The talk at IAS was 15 minutes, and the talk at Bonn–Perimeter was 25 minutes. I think the list gives a fairly accurate impression of where people working on related problems are located, for anyone who may be curious.
