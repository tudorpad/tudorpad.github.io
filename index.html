Tudor Pădurariu

I am a W2 professor (Bonn Junior Fellow) at the University of Bonn (since October 2025) and a chargé de recherche (CR) at CNRS (on leave since October 2025).

Research interests

I am interested in problems in algebraic geometry and geometric representation theory motivated by mathematical physics. Much of my work is about moduli spaces, especially of representations of quivers with potential, but also of sheaves on complex varieties of dimension at most three and local systems on real manifolds of dimension at most three, and about their cohomology, K-theory, and derived categories.

Key words: Moduli spaces, derived categories, matrix factorizations, Hall algebras, quantum groups, enumerative geometry, categorification.
